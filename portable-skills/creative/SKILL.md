# Creative Skill

## Purpose
Produce original concepts by using divergent thinking, unusual combinations, metaphorical reasoning, and transforming constraints into creative opportunities while resisting conventional framings.

## When to Use
- Crafting original product concepts, narratives, metaphors, or user experiences
- Finding fresh design or architectural angles when standard patterns feel generic
- Reframing tight technical, budget, or time constraints into distinctive strengths
- Generating multiple distinct artistic, conceptual, or strategic interpretations

## Behavioral Guidelines
1. Approach problems from unusual angles rather than starting with the standard template.
2. Transform constraints (time, budget, platform limits) into creative opportunities.
3. Use metaphors and analogies to unlock fresh insight and communicate complex ideas.
4. Combine disparate domains, disciplines, or aesthetics to create novel solutions.
5. Resist the first "obvious" solution and push past predictable clichés.
6. Generate multiple distinct creative interpretations before settling on one direction.

## Workflow
1. **Identify the Cliché**: Name the most obvious, expected solution so you can intentionally avoid it.
2. **Embrace Constraints**: List the hardest limits and ask how each could become a signature feature.
3. **Draw Analogies**: Map the problem onto an unrelated domain (architecture, music, ecology, theater).
4. **Generate Interpretations**: Sketch 3–5 genuinely different conceptual directions, each with its own voice or mechanism.
5. **Refine the Spark**: Develop the strongest concept with concrete details that preserve its originality.

## Output Expectations
- Original concepts that clearly depart from generic defaults
- Multiple distinct interpretations or creative directions
- Vivid metaphors, analogies, or cross-domain connections
- Concrete ways constraints are turned into advantages

## Limitations
- Originality can conflict with strict compliance, conventions, or familiar UX patterns if unchecked
- Does not guarantee technical feasibility or operational efficiency without follow-up review
- Not suited for tasks requiring strict adherence to rigid procedures

## Example
**Prompt**: "We need an error page for when our developer tool hits a rate limit, but we don't want a boring '429 Too Many Requests' banner."

**Response**:
1. **Avoid the Cliché**: Skip generic red alert boxes.
2. **Reframe Constraint**: The user is forced to wait—turn wait time into a micro-moment of delight or utility.
3. **Three Interpretations**:
   - *The Pit Stop*: A live countdown styled like a Formula 1 telemetry pit stop, showing exact reset time and a 1-click "copy retry curl" button.
   - *The Breathing Terminal*: A calm ASCII progress bar that paces out the cooldown seconds alongside a subtle keyboard shortcut tip.
   - *The Queue Ticket*: A retro deli-counter ticket ("Now serving #41, your token is #42") with transparent queue position.
